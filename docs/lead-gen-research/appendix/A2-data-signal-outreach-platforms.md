## Legal summary for outreach (secondary sources unless noted; check before relying)
- **UK PECR** ([ICO B2B page](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)):
  - Email to *corporate subscribers* (companies, LLPs, Scottish partnerships) needs no prior PECR consent.
  - **Sole traders and non-LLP partnerships count as individuals**, so consent or the soft opt-in applies.
  - UK GDPR still needs a lawful basis, usually legitimate interests ([Warner Goodman](https://www.warnergoodman.co.uk/site/blog/news/gdpr-news-update-on-b2b-communications)).
  - The DUAA 2025 raised the maximum PECR fine from £500k to **£17.5m or 4% of global turnover** ([Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/the-data-use-and-access-act-pecr-reform-rules-relating-to-electronic-marketing-and-cookies-in-the-uk)). Commencement date conflicts: 19 Jun 2025 vs 5 Feb 2026.
- **Germany:** UWG §7 is widely read as needing prior consent even for B2B email ([Overloop](https://overloop.com/blog/b2b-cold-email-germany-gdpr-compliance)). **France:** CNIL allows professional email prospecting if the message relates to the recipient's role and has an opt-out ([LeadGenius](https://www.leadgenius.com/resources/best-b2b-data-provider-for-france-cnil-b2b-carve-outs-and-bloctel-compliance)).
- **US CAN-SPAM:** no prior consent needed; include a postal address and a working opt-out, and honour opt-outs within 10 business days. Penalties reportedly up to ~$53,088 per email (secondary source; check the FTC) ([Instantly blog](https://instantly.ai/blog/b2b-email-list-compliance-gdpr-canspam)).
